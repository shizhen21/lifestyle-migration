YOUR LIFE STAGES

Where were you, and for how long?

Choose an MSA by name, or activate a stage and click its boundary on the map. Each chapter has its own education, employment, marital status and household children. Optional family income can also change between chapters.

Same year, several places? Add each in residence order. Leave months blank for equal shares, or enter months; blank entries split the remainder of 12. Explicit months must fill the year. After that year, the final residence continues until a new chapter. Use “Earlier in order” to reorder same-year stages. Demographic details can differ by stage. COMPARE THE DAILY MINUTES

Each difference is the comparison path minus your track at the selected year or age. It can combine differences in year, age, place and demographics. It is a model comparison, not a causal effect.

What do the six activities include? →

An estimated life-stage profile.

This reconstructs model-estimated days for the profile and places you specify. It is not a panel of your observed diaries, nor a forecast of what a move will cause. The current data end in 2025.

One model, common comparisons.

The full ATUS model controls calendar differences and averages unspecified features over a shared reference population. Metro effects are pooled across years. Ages 85 and older use the model’s 85+ category. Sparse samples and historical geographic changes remain limitations. Model methods · Track methods.
